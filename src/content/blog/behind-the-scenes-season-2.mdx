[Season 1 of the Hey Bible Podcast](https://✝️.fm) is already out in the world: the free World English Bible, narrated and released one book at a time on a steady cadence — Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and [https://✝️.fm](https://✝️.fm).

**Season 2** is what we’re building next to it. Same free Scripture, same “press play and listen” mission, richer production: a warmer HD narrator, classical oil-painting chapter art, and slow Ken Burns video timed to the verses. This is the light product preview. For the full workshop write-up — chunking, ffmpeg, cron jobs, the scars we kept — see Moses’s post: [Hey Bible Podcast Season 2: Behind the Scenes](https://moses.codes/blog/hey-bible-podcast-season-2-behind-the-scenes).

We’re far enough along to share a real chapter cut, not a mood board.

What’s different is less a single feature and more a small factory. Nightly, Venice generates verse audio with MiniMax HD **DeepVoiceMan** — warmer and fuller than Season 1’s Bill track — and we keep every verse MP3 forever, stitching chapter audio only when a chapter is complete. A little later, a second job picks up chapters that already have audio: it groups verses into ~18–30 second chunks on verse boundaries, paints an oil still per chunk with **Grok Imagine**, animates each still with a gentle Ken Burns zoom and pan, crossfades the segments, and lays the chapter narration underneath. Two jobs on purpose so a long video render never blocks tomorrow’s verses.

We tried photoreal landscapes along the way. They felt like stock footage next to the text. The look we locked is quieter: classical European **oil painting**, wide 16:9 plates, no frames or borders, motion slow enough to sit with — more museum wing than trailer. No drama tracks. No multi-voice cast. Just clear Scripture and pictures that move at the pace of the words.

Season 2 doesn’t launch publicly until Season 1 finishes all **66 books**. We’re generating S2 in parallel so that day isn’t a cold start; S1 keeps its Sunday release rhythm, and S2 stays behind a release gate until that finish line.

Until then, Season 1 is ready whenever you are — [https://✝️.fm](https://✝️.fm), [podcast.heybible.org](https://podcast.heybible.org), or *The Hey Bible Podcast* on Spotify and Apple. And if you want the engineering deep dive, it’s all on [moses.codes](https://moses.codes/blog/hey-bible-podcast-season-2-behind-the-scenes).

Same mission as always: a free Bible you can press play on. Season 2 just adds pictures that move with the words.
