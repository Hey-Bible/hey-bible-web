## A real chapter, not a mockup

We’re far enough along to share real output, not a mood board. Genesis 1 is already a finished chapter cut: oil plates, slow motion, and the full narration underneath.

What’s new in Season 2 is less a single switch and more a small stack working together. The narrator is MiniMax HD **DeepVoiceMan** through Venice — warmer and fuller than Season 1’s Bill track. Chapter art is classical European old-master **oil painting**, stills from **Grok Imagine**, wide and quiet rather than flashy. On top of that, each chapter becomes **video**: slow **Ken Burns** motion across those plates, timed to the verse audio so the picture changes with the reading instead of on an arbitrary clock. And the work is split into **two nightly jobs** — audio first, then photos and video — so a long render never blocks tomorrow’s verses.

## How the factory works

**Job 1 runs in the evening and handles audio.** It generates verse MP3s with Venice TTS, keeps every verse forever (the permanent library rule from Season 1 still stands), and stitches chapter audio only when a chapter’s verses are complete. Hundreds of verses can land in a good night; if a file already exists, we skip it and move on.

**Job 2 starts a little later and handles media.** For chapters that already have audio, it groups verses into roughly **18–30 second** chunks on verse boundaries, paints an oil still per chunk, animates each still with a gentle Ken Burns zoom and pan, crossfades the segments, and muxes the chapter narration underneath. No drama tracks. No multi-voice cast. Just clear Scripture, gallery-quiet art, and motion slow enough to sit with.

That split is intentional. Early on we tried doing speech and long ffmpeg in one sitting; the video side is simply too slow and too easy to interrupt. Audio stays a fast, repeatable batch. Media grinds after the narration has already earned its chapter file.

## Style choices

We tried photoreal landscapes. They felt like stock footage next to the text — too “travel channel,” not enough “this was made on purpose.”

The lock we kept is quieter:

- Classical **oil painting** look  
- Wide **16:9** plates  
- **No frames or borders**  
- Gentle motion (smooth ease, not whip-pans)

It should feel like walking a museum wing while someone reads Genesis — not like a trailer. Prompts stay content-aware so the art follows the passage, and if a still gets blocked we fall back to a safer landscape rather than stalling the whole chapter.

## When Season 2 launches

**After Season 1 finishes all 66 books.**

We’re generating S2 in parallel so launch isn’t a cold start. Season 1 keeps its Sunday release rhythm; Season 2 stays behind a release gate until that finish line. We won’t overwrite S1 library objects or confuse the feed people are already subscribed to — when S2 ships, it should feel like a true second season of the same show, not a replacement.
