Hey Bible looks different today. The sky blue is gone. In its place is a warm cream page, soft brown ink, and cards cut out like stickers with a white edge around them.

We call it Storybook Sticker. We wanted opening the app to feel like opening a favorite picture book: fun, friendly, and easy for anyone to pick up.

## What changed

- **Color.** A cream page with brown ink, and a small set of soft colors beside it: terracotta, sage, rose, sky and wheat.
- **Stickers.** Verses, notes and chats sit on cards with a white edge and a soft shadow, as if they were stuck onto the page. A few hand-drawn friends turn up along the way. Look out for the sheep.
- **Type.** Headings are set in Fredoka, a rounded typeface. Body text stays in Nunito, so reading a verse feels the same as before.
- **Night.** Dark mode is the same sticker sheet at night: a deep blue sky, cream text, wheat headings and a scattering of stars.
- **Logo.** The book-and-cross mark is redrawn to match, with a new wordmark beside it.

## Verse images

There is a new default style for verse images, called Hey Bible. It draws the scene from your verse as sticker art on a cream page. The verse sits below it on its own cream plate, clear of the picture, with the reference above the verse and the translation's copyright line beneath it.

Every other style is still there. If you love your watercolors or your stained glass, pick them from the style list as before.

## Everywhere, not just the app

The new look is not only in the app. This website has it, and so do the [developer docs](https://docs.heybible.app) and the [Hey Bible Podcast](https://podcast.heybible.org) site. Our pages on [X](https://x.com/heybibleapp), [Instagram](https://instagram.com/heybibleapp) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/heybibleapp/) are changing too.

## How it was made

The look started as a written art direction: the palette, the line weight, the white sticker edge, and what to leave out. Every screen and every picture was then held to it. If you want to know why a written spec works better than a clever prompt when you make images with AI, Working Dev's Hero wrote about it in [Stop Prompting, Start Art Directing](https://workingdevshero.com/blog/art-direction-beats-prompts).

## Try it

Update the app, or open [heybible.app](https://heybible.app), and have a look around. Nothing has moved: search, favorites, notes, chats and images are all where you left them.
