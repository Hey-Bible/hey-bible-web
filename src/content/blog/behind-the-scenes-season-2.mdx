## How the factory works (short version)

**Job 1 — Audio (evening)**  
Generate verse MP3s with Venice TTS, keep every verse forever, stitch chapter audio when a chapter is complete.

**Job 2 — Media (a little later)**  
For chapters that already have audio: group verses into ~18–30s chunks, paint an oil still per chunk, animate each still with a smooth Ken Burns zoom/pan, crossfade the segments, and mux the chapter audio underneath.

That’s it. No drama tracks. No multi-voice cast. Just clear Scripture, gallery-quiet art, and motion slow enough to sit with.

## Style choices

We tried photoreal landscapes. They felt like stock footage next to the text.

The lock we kept:

- Classical **oil painting** look  
- Wide 16:9 plates  
- No frames or borders  
- Gentle motion (smooth ease, not whip-pans)

It should feel like walking a museum wing while someone reads Genesis — not like a trailer.

## When does Season 2 launch?

**After Season 1 finishes all 66 books.**

We’re generating S2 in parallel so launch isn’t a cold start. S1 keeps its Sunday release rhythm; S2 stays behind a release gate until that finish line.
