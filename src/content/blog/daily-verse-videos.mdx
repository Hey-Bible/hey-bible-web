Every morning at 7 we post a short verse video. No sermon. Just a landscape, a little music, and Scripture.

Those clips used to be picture-only. The verse lived in the caption. Beautiful, but easy to miss with the sound off, and easy to forget which verse you had just seen.

This week we put the words **on** the film.

## What changed

The picture still comes first. About half a second of quiet, then a card **flips in** toward you (a rotate around the X axis, like a page turning up from the horizon). On it:

- the reference in gold, with CSB
- a thin gold rule
- the verse in warm cream
- a dark, see-through plate so the hills stay visible

In the corner, small and quiet: **@heybibleapp**.

We tried this first on [Philippians 4:13](https://files.wdh.sh/download/f0a6d0260ad06ab96b6ef9d8) (landscape) and the same cut in [9:16](https://files.wdh.sh/download/d9685878de5f2b3b0776a17b). That is the lock for new daily videos, and we are walking back through the older ones one night at a time.

This is a different lane from [Season 2 of the podcast](/blog/behind-the-scenes-season-2). Those chapter films are old-master oil and Ken Burns. These daily posts are photoreal morning light, made to sit next to everything else in a social feed.

## Why the app should match

Hey Bible already lets you generate a verse image in the app. Until now that overlay was a wide white band with black type. Same mission, different face.

If the videos and the in-app art disagree, the product feels like two teams. So we filed [issue #260](https://github.com/Hey-Bible/hey-bible-app/issues/260) and opened [PR #261](https://github.com/Hey-Bible/hey-bible-app/pull/261) to restyle the image generator to this card: dark plate, gold reference, cream verse, gold rule, same small watermark. Stills cannot flip, but they can wear the same clothes.

## What we learned making the videos

- **Match the music to the still**, not to a default piano patch. A sunrise meadow wants guitar. A woods path does not want brass.
- **Keep motion slow and even.** The first 15 seconds and the extra 10 have to feel like one shot.
- **Put the words on last.** Generate the living picture, then set type. Do not ask the image model to spell.
- **One verse a day, 7 AM Eastern.** The factory can run at night. People should wake up to one quiet thing.

If you follow [@heybibleapp](https://instagram.com/heybibleapp), you will start seeing the card on new posts. In the app, new verse images will follow as soon as that PR lands.
